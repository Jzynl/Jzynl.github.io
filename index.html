Case study · Business workflow automation

LARA Tours & Travels

Improving the website and building the operational dashboard behind the client experience.

The situation

Travel staff needed to prepare itineraries and client-facing documents, manage booking information, and keep website content current. Some document work required repeated manual preparation and branding corrections.

My contribution
  • Improved the existing public website.
  • Developed an internal dashboard for booking management and daily operations.
  • Automated itinerary and proposal generation.
  • Implemented generation of invoices, receipts and official letterheads.
  • Added content management for tours, destinations, articles and offers.
  • Supported digital marketing strategy and promotional content.
The approach

The goal was to address recurring operational needs instead of building features without a clear purpose. I focused on connecting booking information to the documents staff needed, and on making content editable by staff without a developer having to publish every update.

The system should remove repetitive work, improve consistency, and let staff focus on the customer.
Outcome

Itinerary preparation was reduced from hours to under a minute. Staff could manage key website content independently, and generated documents became more consistent with the company's branding.

Case study · Business operations

Serengeti Suites

Turning a public booking website into a connected hotel operations system.

Problem

Hotel operations involved bookings, room readiness, housekeeping, payment records and daily financial information. Staff needed a shared view of operational information rather than relying entirely on paper and separate conversations.

My contribution

Designed, developed and deployed a booking website and staff dashboard, then continued to improve the system around operational needs.

  • Booking requests and staff booking management.
  • Room availability and occupancy views.
  • Housekeeping inspections and room readiness.
  • Payments, expenses, refunds and financial reporting.
  • Staff roles, database design and production deployment.
Implementation

Django, PostgreSQL, Gunicorn and Nginx on Linux infrastructure. The system is maintained in small iterations as operational needs become clear.

Case study · Applied AI

Hotel RAG

A document-grounded guest assistant designed to retrieve information, cite sources and handle repeat questions efficiently.

Problem

Guests ask repetitive questions about hotel services and policies. Answers need to be grounded in the hotel's own documents rather than generated from unsupported assumptions.

Implementation
  • Document retrieval using embeddings and vector search.
  • PostgreSQL with pgvector for retrieval.
  • FastAPI request handling and background Celery tasks.
  • Redis queue and caching for repeated queries.
  • Query logging and a repeatable evaluation set.
  • Docker Compose and deployment tooling.
RAGFastAPIpgvector RedisCeleryDocker
Engineering focus

Beyond connecting a model, the project focuses on retrieval quality, response latency, logging and evaluation so that changes can be tested rather than judged only by appearance.

About

How I think about building.

I build technology around people, not the other way around.

I'm a Computer Engineer and full-stack developer in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. I work across web applications, business systems, interfaces, applied AI and deployment.

My starting point is the problem: what are people trying to do, where does their current process break down, and what would make the work simpler or more reliable?

I like understanding how the parts fit together. A form, the database it writes to, the report it feeds and the server it runs on are one connected system.

The finish line isn't just code that works or an interface that looks right. It's a person using the system to do what they came to do without fighting the software.
